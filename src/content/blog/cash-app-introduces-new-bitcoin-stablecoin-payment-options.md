<p>

</p>

<p>

<h3>How Lightning Network Actually Works</h3>

<p>
Here's the simple breakdown: Lightning Network creates payment channels on top of Bitcoin that can process thousands of transactions per second. Instead of waiting for the main Bitcoin network to confirm your transaction (which can take 10+ minutes), Lightning transactions confirm instantly.</p>

<p>
The technical details are complex, but for you as a user, it's incredibly simple: You scan a QR code, choose your payment method, and boom – instant payment. No waiting, no high fees, no complicated crypto addresses to deal with.
</p>
